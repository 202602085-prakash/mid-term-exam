Guide to Comparing Domestic Coffee Franchises

Three Korean coffee brands, one shared measure: the number of franchise stores at the end of 2025. Compare like with like, check the source, and remember that more stores do not automatically mean higher profit.

Created by Prakash Tamang · Research page · October 2026

1. Comparison method

This page compares Mega MGC Coffee, Compose Coffee, and Paik’s Coffee in the same industry. The selected metric is franchised outlet count at year-end 2025, measured in stores. This is a scale measure, not a sales or profitability measure.

Important data note: Published reports can differ slightly because they may use different reporting dates or definitions. The values below use a secondary news analysis citing Korea Fair Trade Commission disclosure data for the 2025 reporting year. Definitions and filing coverage can vary, so verify the original disclosure documents. Treat them as reported figures and verify them against the official franchise disclosure system before making a business decision.

한국어: 이 페이지는 같은 업종인 메가MGC커피, 컴포즈커피, 빽다방을 비교합니다. 비교 기준은 2025년 말 가맹점 수이며 단위는 ‘개’입니다. 매장 수는 사업 규모를 보여 주지만 매출이나 수익성을 직접 의미하지는 않습니다.

4. What can we conclude?

  • Mega MGC Coffee has the largest reported franchise network of the three.
  • Compose Coffee is second, and Paik’s Coffee is third, using this source and this base year.
  • Store count alone cannot tell us average store revenue, operating costs, rent, closure risk, or owner profit.
  • Before investing, compare the same base year for average annual sales per franchise store, startup costs, royalties, lease costs, and local competition.

한국어: 이 자료에서는 메가MGC커피, 컴포즈커피, 빽다방 순으로 가맹점 수가 많습니다. 하지만 점포 수만으로 매출, 비용, 폐점 위험 또는 점주의 이익을 알 수 없습니다. 실제 창업 전에는 정보공개서와 상권 자료를 함께 확인해야 합니다.

  1. Domestic franchise revenue rankings
    Broad search: usually returns a mix of news, lists, and different years or metrics.
  2. Domestic franchise revenue rankings filetype:pdf
    PDF filter: can surface formal reports and downloadable documents, but the base year still needs checking.
  3. Domestic franchise revenue rankings site:naver.com
    Domain filter: narrows results to Naver-hosted pages; it does not guarantee official or comparable data.
  4. Domestic franchise revenue rankings after:2024-06-01 before:2026-01-01
    Date filter: prioritizes pages published in the chosen period. The page publication date may differ from the year the figures describe.

Research lesson: Search results change when you add file type, website, or date filters. Always record both the publication date and the data’s base year. This page uses outlet counts rather than revenue because the cited report gives same-year figures for all three brands.

한국어: 검색어에 PDF, 사이트, 날짜 조건을 추가하면 결과가 달라집니다. 게시일과 실제 통계 기준 연도를 구분해야 합니다. 이 페이지는 세 브랜드를 같은 연도 기준으로 비교할 수 있도록 가맹점 수를 사용합니다.

  3. Korea FTC Franchise Business Transaction information portal (official franchise disclosure lookup; search each brand’s disclosure document)
  4. Yonhap News — Coffee franchises in South Korea rise in 2022 (useful context only; different base year)
